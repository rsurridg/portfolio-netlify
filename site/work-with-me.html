Frequently asked questions

I want to commission a painting or artwork — what are the next steps?

To put together a proposal for you, there are four things I'll need to know. Include as much of this as you can when you reach out:

  1. Digital or traditional? If traditional, which medium? The choice of medium significantly impacts both the timeline and the pricing of a commission.
  2. Do you have a reference photo? That's totally okay if not — I'm happy to work from an idea and find my own references. If you do provide a reference, you must have rights to the image (or express written permission from the photographer). Reference quality matters: high resolution, natural lighting, and minimal lens distortion will produce the best result.
  3. Matting and framing? (Traditional pieces only.) If you'd like me to handle matting and framing, I can include options and an estimated cost in your quote.
  4. Size preference? (Traditional pieces only.) Sometimes size is dictated by the reference and the medium, but if you're trying to fill a specific space, let me know!

Once I have that, I'll put together a proposal and we can go from there.

What do you charge for a website?

I bill an hourly flat fee for website development and maintenance. I can also manage your domain and hosting setup for an annual fee.

I specialize in static portfolio sites for creators that carry a minimal monthly or recurring cost. This site, for example, costs $8/year for the domain and $0 for hosting.

Different website solutions have different costs and benefits — reach out and I'm happy to walk you through the options and put together a quote based on what you need.

Do you use AI?

For visual art, content writing, and editing: no, not at any stage.

In the domain of fine arts and literature, I believe that when we sacrifice the creative process on the altar of expedience, our message loses its credibility entirely. Therefore, no AI is involved in the ideation or realization of any of my own art and prose. When I create a piece of visual art (whether digital or traditional):

  • I am manually gathering and compositing references — (to the best of my knowledge, I will always avoid usage of AI-generated reference images)
  • I do not make use of any AI tools to assist in the process of creation. For digital art, I work primarily in Procreate, which has zero AI integrations, and my paintings are entirely human-made, stroke-for-stroke

When I'm writing, editing, and formatting (my own work or yours):

  • I do not use AI tools to write, analyze, suggest, or format
  • Your work will never be exposed to a bot

For web development: yes, but with limits.

I do believe there is room for AI tools in the realm of software engineering. Code generation with AI assistance allows me to put together websites at lower cost to you (because I'm spending fewer hours developing the site layout and the software architecture behind it). I've been a web developer much longer than generative AI has been commercially available, and code cleanliness, performance, and maintainability are important to me when designing your site.

  • I do not compromise good development practices when it comes to AI usage
  • I will not expose your content to an AI agent without express permission. I am more than happy to create your site template using placeholder content, and then manually go in and wire up your personal content, so that the bot never sees it.
  • I will not use AI to generate your website's content unless you specifically request it.
What mediums do you work in?

Traditional: Graphite on paper, oil on canvas, watercolor, chalk pastel, and mixed media.

Digital: Procreate on iPad. All digital work is painted stroke-for-stroke — no generative tools or filters.

If you have a specific medium in mind that's not listed here, feel free to ask — I'm always happy to discuss.

Do you ship original works?

Yes — I can ship original traditional pieces. Shipping cost and handling will be included in your commission quote. If you'd like matting and framing handled before shipment, I can include that as well.

Digital commissions are delivered as high-resolution files.

Can I use my commissioned artwork commercially?

Standard commissions are for personal use only. Commercial licensing (for products, branding, publications, etc.) is available at an additional fee — just mention it when you reach out so I can include it in your quote.
